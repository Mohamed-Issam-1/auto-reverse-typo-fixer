# Dictionary data license

`ar.fst` and `en.fst` are compiled from the frequency lists `ar_50k.txt` and `en_50k.txt` of the
**FrequencyWords** project by Hermit Dave (https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), generated
from the OpenSubtitles corpus. Words were normalized, filtered (letters only, no triple repeats) and
truncated to the most frequent entries.
